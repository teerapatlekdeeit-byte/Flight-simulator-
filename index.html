GAMEPAD
Pad sticks fly · RT/LT throttle · A brake/guns · B gear · X flaps · Y camera · RB missile

Takeoff: full throttle, keep straight with A/D, rotate at about Cessna 110 · A320 270 · Fighter 260 · Jumbo 295 · Hypersonic 400 km/h, then raise the gear with G. Landing: gear down, flaps, aim for the runway numbers, cut power before touchdown and flare. Aim for under 300 fpm on the centerline for a high score.
